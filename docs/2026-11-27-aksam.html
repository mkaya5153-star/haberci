AI-based digital pathology defines spatial border ecosystems in triple-negative breast cancer.AI-based digital pathology defines spatial border ecosystems in triple-negative breast cancer.

Science (New York, N.Y.) · Zhang H, Yang F, Tian P, et al.

TÜRKÇE

The invasive tumor border in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) is currently poorly understood. We integrated digital pathology and spatial multi-omics to define border ecosystems in TNBC. AI-based digital pathology identified five border ecosystems, three associated with prognosis. Spatial multi-omics analyses further resolved the spatial niche, molecular mechanisms and clinical relevance of these ecosystems. Immune-infiltrated borders contain a triadic immune niche associated with favorable prognosis and immunotherapy sensitivity. Tumor-stroma borders harbor a MIF+ fibroblast exclusion niche that blocks immune infiltration and suppresses T cells function, predicting poor prognosis.

Middle-Late Pleistocene Denisovans and unresolved Homo in southern China.Middle-Late Pleistocene Denisovans and unresolved Homo in southern China.

Nature · Xing S, Rao H, Xie G, et al.

TÜRKÇE

Molecular and morphological evidence increasingly supports the view that most East Asian Middle to Late Pleistocene archaic Homo belong to Denisovans1, yet the presence of other contemporaneous lineages has remained unclear. Here we report a set of newly discovered hominin fossils (4 isolated teeth; more than 100.8 thousand years ago and 104.5-42.6 thousand years ago, respectively) from 2 cave sites (Chuanshandong and Longchuandong) in Guangxi, southern China. Integrated morphological and palaeoproteomic analyses reveal substantial phenotypic and molecular diversity consistent with at least two distinct lineages.
