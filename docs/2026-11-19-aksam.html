Transient contacts between filaments impart its elasticity to branched actin.Transient contacts between filaments impart its elasticity to branched actin.

Nature · Bouzid M, Valencia-Gallardo C, Kopec M, et al.

TÜRKÇE

Cells dynamically assemble actin into branched networks to push on their environment during cell migration, endocytosis or the maintenance of cell-cell contact1. These networks are among the stiffest found in the cytoskeleton and can generate large pushing stresses against stationary obstacles2-5. When grown against a controlled opposing stress in an atomic force microscope6 or inside the cell7, branched networks alter their own architecture to adapt their compressional stiffness to their environment, but the underpinnings of their mechanics and adaptability are unclear.

Bogong moths navigate with a light-dependent geomagnetic inclination compass.Bogong moths navigate with a light-dependent geomagnetic inclination compass.

Nature · Chen H, Rosberg L, Dreyer D, et al.

TÜRKÇE

Bogong moths (Agrotis infusa) migrate biannually up to 1,000 km between their breeding areas and geographically restricted aestivation sites in the Australian Alps1,2. These moths navigate towards their distant goal using the stars as a compass3 but navigate correctly even when the stars are obscured by clouds3, suggesting that their magnetic sense4 probably harnesses the Earth's magnetic field as a compass. However, the nature of this compass, and of the magnetic sense underlying it, remains unknown. Here we show that Bogong moths, tethered in a flight arena under a full-spectrum randomized starry night sky, navigate in their inherited migratory direction using the geomagnetic field alone.

Evidence for p-wave magnetism in electronic band structure of metallic EuSi.Evidence for p-wave magnetism in electronic band structure of metallic EuSi.

Nature · Cheng E, Yang Y, Gao Y, et al.

TÜRKÇE

One active frontier of quantum-matter research is the realization of p-wave symmetry-breaking phases in fermionic systems, long exemplified by the A phase of superfluid helium-3 (refs. 1-3). Achieving p-wave superconductivity4 and its magnetic analogue5-characterized by a time-reversal-symmetric, p-wave spin-polarized Fermi surface-remains a grand challenge6,7. Despite recent progress in identifying candidate p-wave magnets5,7-10, spin-resolved spectroscopic evidence for their electronic structures remains limited.
