Myelin pathology is a key feature of X-linked dystonia-parkinsonism.Myelin pathology is a key feature of X-linked dystonia-parkinsonism.

Cell · Prakash P, Limberg KC, Zhang W, et al.

TÜRKÇE

X-linked dystonia-parkinsonism (XDP) is an adult-onset movement disorder predominantly affecting males of Filipino descent. It is caused by the insertion of a SINE-variable number of tandem repeat (VNTR)-Alu subfamily F (SVA_F) retrotransposon in the TATA-box binding protein-associated factor 1 (TAF1) gene. A barrier to XDP research has been the lack of animal models. Here, we introduce a conditional humanized XDP mouse model harboring a hybrid mouse-human Taf1/TAF1 gene (hyTAF1) containing the pathogenic SVA_F. We activated hyTAF1 in Nestin+ cells and found the resulting XDP male mice recapitulate features of human XDP, including severe motor impairment and striatal atrophy.

Analogue simulation of massive relativistic quantum fields in 2 + 1 dimensions.Analogue simulation of massive relativistic quantum fields in 2 + 1 dimensions.

Nature · Zhang Y, Wang F, Wong PHC, et al.

TÜRKÇE

Quantum field theories provide fundamental models of complex interacting systems1-3, from high-energy physics and cosmology to condensed matter. However, solving these models in non-perturbative and dynamical regimes is often challenging, particularly in more than one spatial dimension. Analogue simulation using tunable synthetic quantum systems can both verify existing theoretical predictions and lead to physical insights. Here we realize analogue simulation of massive relativistic quantum fields in 2 + 1 dimensions (two spatial dimensions and time), using two coherently coupled spin components4,5 in a uniform two-dimensional atomic Bose-Einstein condensate6,7.
