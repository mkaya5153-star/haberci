Cost-Effectiveness of Alcohol Use Disorder Treatment for Alcohol-Associated Liver Disease.Cost-Effectiveness of Alcohol Use Disorder Treatment for Alcohol-Associated Liver Disease.

JAMA network open · Gerber A, Agyekum A, Yang JY, et al.

TÜRKÇE

IMPORTANCE: Alcohol-associated liver disease (ALD) is a leading cause of liver-related mortality and liver transplant. Combined behavioral interventions (CBI) and medications for alcohol use disorder (MAUD) are underutilized. Thus, it is critical to identify optimal therapeutic and cost-effective windows for CBI and MAUD in patients with ALD at earlier fibrosis stages. OBJECTIVE: To evaluate the cost-effectiveness of naltrexone and CBI in preventing cirrhosis in patients with ALD compared with no treatment. DESIGN, SETTING, AND PARTICIPANTS: This economic evaluation used a Markov state-transition model simulating lifetime disease progression and health care costs associated with ALD.
