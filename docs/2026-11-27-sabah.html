Integrating established and emerging tools to assess wildlife condition from the ground, up.Integrating established and emerging tools to assess wildlife condition from the ground, up.

Science (New York, N.Y.) · Review · Shero MR, Johnston DW, Kirkham AL, et al.

TÜRKÇE

Metabolic energy links individual physiology to population-level dynamics, making wildlife body condition a cornerstone of conservation science. Emerging technologies, including unoccupied aircraft systems, make remote assessment of animal condition possible at unprecedented scales, and physiologically grounded validation is essential for strong inference. Here we examine the strengths and challenges of traditional and remote methods, using a comparative perspective that emphasizes both lipid and lean mass dynamics in evaluating wildlife health.

Nature · Finocchio G, Oberli S, Lampe GD, et al.

TÜRKÇE

CRISPR-associated transposases (CASTs) achieve site-specific DNA integration by combining the RNA-guided targeting action of a nuclease-deficient CRISPR-Cas system with the assembly of a Tn7-like transpososome complex1,2. Understanding the detailed mechanisms of this elaborate process is paramount to engineering CAST systems into programmable genetic tools3-6. JAMA network open · Consensus Statement · Shah VN, Bannuru RR, Durnwald C, et al.

TÜRKÇE

IMPORTANCE: Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA), although preventable in most cases, is a leading cause of hospitalization in people with insulin-treated diabetes and is associated with suboptimal glycemic outcomes, increased risk for diabetes complications, and increased health care expenditure. This consensus statement outlines factors that may help identify individuals with diabetes who are at increased risk for elevated ketone levels; provides recommendations for ketone monitoring, including potential testing modalities; and discusses appropriate testing frequency and self-management strategies based on ketone severity.
