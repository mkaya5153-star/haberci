Circulation · Clinical Trial · Fang J, Chen Y, Zhou W, et al.

TÜRKÇE

BACKGROUND: Nonobstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (nHCM), defined as hypertrophic cardiomyopathy without left ventricular outflow tract obstruction, remains challenging to treat because of its heterogeneous pathophysiology. This study evaluated the safety and efficacy of transapical beating-heart septal myectomy in patients with symptomatic nHCM. METHODS: Patients with symptomatic, drug-refractory nHCM were prospectively enrolled to undergo transapical beating-heart septal myectomy through a left minithoracotomy.

Architecture of a portal complex embedded in the poxvirus core.Architecture of a portal complex embedded in the poxvirus core.

Nature · Hong Y, Liu S, Wang K, et al.

TÜRKÇE

Gene transcription within the viral core is a distinctive feature of poxvirus replication. Following entry into the cytoplasm, the poxvirus core dissociates from the lateral bodies and undergoes expansion, functioning as a compartment for early transcription1. However, how molecules are exchanged between the viral core and the host cytoplasm remains poorly understood. Here, using cryo-electron tomography and sub-tomogram averaging, we determine the structures of the portal complex and its central pore on the vaccinia virus core at 6.7 Å and 4.4 Å resolution, respectively.
