Neural correlates of facial recognition in a social wasp.Neural correlates of facial recognition in a social wasp.

Cell · Jernigan CM, Mammen LCC, Freiwald WA, et al.

TÜRKÇE

In humans and other primates, faces are special visual objects that are encoded by circuits selective to faces. If specialized circuits are the most efficient way to encode and discriminate among similar-looking objects of high relevance, then we expect that analogous circuits should evolve in other taxa with specialized visual individual recognition abilities. Thus, we surveyed the neural responses to visual objects across the brain of a wasp that has independently evolved specialized facial recognition, Polistes fuscatus. We found a localized population of neurons analogous to face cells, which we term "wasp cells," that selectively fire to front-facing wasp images.

VAD Wean Recovery Network: Long-Term Outcomes of LVAD Weaning for Myocardial Recovery.VAD Wean Recovery Network: Long-Term Outcomes of LVAD Weaning for Myocardial Recovery.

Circulation · Patel SR, Potapov E, Kanwar M, et al.

TÜRKÇE

BACKGROUND: Myocardial recovery facilitated by the combination of a left ventricular assist device (LVAD) and guideline-directed medical therapy can lead to sufficient myocardial improvement. This in turn may allow LVAD weaning in appropriately selected patients with chronic advanced heart failure. However, sufficient data on the long-term outcomes of this therapeutic strategy are lacking, thereby hindering widespread adoption of LVAD weaning. We present the primary results from the VAD Wean Myocardial Recovery International Network examining long-term outcomes after LVAD weaning for myocardial recovery.

NR6A1 Drives Cardiac Remodeling by Transcriptionally Activating the Mineralocorticoid Receptor.NR6A1 Drives Cardiac Remodeling by Transcriptionally Activating the Mineralocorticoid Receptor.

Circulation · Zhang Z, Zhuang H, Chang X, et al.

TÜRKÇE

BACKGROUND: Activation of the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS) and mineralocorticoid receptor (MR/NR3C2) signaling is a central driver of pathologic cardiac remodeling and heart failure (HF). The transcriptional regulation of NR3C2 and the amplification of RAAS-driven cardiac remodeling remain poorly understood. METHODS: Transcriptome and single-cell RNA sequencing data, along with HF samples from humans and mice, were used for gene expression analysis. Cardiomyocyte-specific Nr6a1 transgenic (Nr6a1-cTG) and knockout (Nr6a1-cKO) mice were subjected to transverse aortic constriction to evaluate cardiac remodeling and dysfunction.
