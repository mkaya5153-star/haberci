TÜRKÇE

IMPORTANCE: Resistance exercise training (RET) has established antidepressant effects compared with inactive control conditions; however, its effects vs active comparisons remain unclear. OBJECTIVE: To quantify the association of RET efficacy with active and inactive comparisons for depressive symptoms and explore variability across moderators. DATA SOURCES: Published articles were identified using Google Scholar, MEDLINE, PubMed, Web of Science, and PsycInfo from inception to July 7, 2025. STUDY SELECTION: Randomized clinical trials (RCTs) with at least 3 weeks of RET or any non-RET comparison and a validated depressive symptom measure at baseline and at least 3 weeks were included.
