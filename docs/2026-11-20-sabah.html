Balancing redox-mediated cytotoxicity and catalysis in natural product biosynthesis.Balancing redox-mediated cytotoxicity and catalysis in natural product biosynthesis.

Science (New York, N.Y.) · Boesger HA, Fang E, Wang R, et al.

TÜRKÇE

Redox-mediated chemistry is central to both the biosynthesis and cytotoxicity of many natural products, yet how microbes balance these processes remains poorly understood. Here, we uncover a conserved mechanism that coordinates three redox-mediated processes in secondary metabolite biosynthesis: self-protection, prevention of substrate degradation, and late-stage biosynthesis. Using the antitumor antibiotic doxorubicin as a model system, we show that the vicinal oxygen chelate (VOC) protein DnrV prevents reactive oxygen species formation and substrate degradation while promoting P450 catalysis.

JAMA network open · Obara T, Naito H, Nojima T, et al.

TÜRKÇE

IMPORTANCE: Compression-only cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CO-CPR) has been widely promoted to encourage bystander intervention in out-of-hospital cardiac arrest (OHCA), but long-term trends in bystander CPR and the associations between CPR type and outcomes in pediatric OHCA remain unclear. OBJECTIVE: To evaluate temporal trends in bystander CPR type and the associations with clinical outcomes among children with OHCA in Japan. DESIGN, SETTING, AND PARTICIPANTS: This cohort study included patients aged 17 years or younger with known receipt of bystander CPR documented in the nationwide All-Japan Utstein Registry from January 1, 2005, to December 31, 2023.

Elastic-field reorganization stabilizes soft-lattice perovskites for durable solar cells.Elastic-field reorganization stabilizes soft-lattice perovskites for durable solar cells.

Science (New York, N.Y.) · Li B, Zhang T, Wang X, et al.

TÜRKÇE

The soft lattice that enables the exceptional performance of halide perovskites inherently compromises their operational stability. Here, we show that soft-lattice perovskites can be stabilized through elastic-field reorganization induced by rigid nanodiamond inclusions. Their inclusion enabled efficient bulk-to-boundary load transfer, redistributing and dissipating internal elastic fields at grain boundaries rather than allowing their accumulation within the crystal interior. Suppression of intergranular sliding constrained lattice deformation and mitigated defect generation during prolonged operation.
