Milk Fat Globule Membrane-Enriched Formula and Cognition Among Formula-Fed Infants: A Randomized Clinical Trial.Milk Fat Globule Membrane-Enriched Formula and Cognition Among Formula-Fed Infants: A Randomized Clinical Trial.

JAMA network open · Equivalence Trial, Randomized Controlled Trial · Gould JF, Gibson RA, Yelland LN, et al.

TÜRKÇE

IMPORTANCE: The absence of milk fat globule membrane (MFGM) in infant formula may contribute to the differences between breastfed and formula-fed infants. OBJECTIVE: To test whether MFGM-enriched infant formula benefits cognitive development of exclusively formula-fed infants. DESIGN, SETTING, AND PARTICIPANTS: This clinician-blinded, researcher-blinded, and participant-blinded randomized clinical trial of exclusively formula-fed infants with a reference group (exclusively breastfed) included full-term singleton infants under 2 months of age, who were recruited from the community (May 2020 to April 2022). Follow-up was completed in June 2024 and analyses completed in September 2025.

Atmospheric organic nitrogen is a major nutrient source to global oceans.Atmospheric organic nitrogen is a major nutrient source to global oceans.

Science (New York, N.Y.) · Yu X, Li Y, Shi G, et al.

TÜRKÇE

Atmospheric nitrogen (N) deposition is a critical nutrient source for marine ecosystems, but its organic nitrogen (ON) component remains poorly quantified. We measured ON in marine aerosols collected during 21 research cruises transecting multiple ocean basins and both hemispheric high latitudes. We used these observations to constrain and evaluate an atmospheric chemistry model with updated ON sources and sinks, enabling global mapping of ON deposition. The model estimated annual ON deposition to oceans at 12.8 (4.0 to 22.7) teragrams of nitrogen per year (Tg N year-1), contributing 27% (11 to 40%) of total N deposition.
