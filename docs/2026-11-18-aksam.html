Degenerative Mitral Regurgitation: From Prolapse to Precision Therapy.Degenerative Mitral Regurgitation: From Prolapse to Precision Therapy.

Circulation · Review · Chikwe J, Delling FN, Gelijns AC

TÜRKÇE

Degenerative mitral valve regurgitation due to mitral valve prolapse affects ≈15 million people globally and is the commonest indication for mitral intervention in high-income countries, but its biology, natural history, and lifetime management are ill defined. This review summarizes current knowledge of the epidemiology, pathophysiology, diagnosis, risk stratification, and lifetime management of degenerative mitral regurgitation, with a focus on areas of uncertainty, recent advances, and emerging research directions.

Paleogenomics illuminates mobility and changing lifeways in Chalcolithic Anatolia.Paleogenomics illuminates mobility and changing lifeways in Chalcolithic Anatolia.

Cell · Aydoğan A, Vural KB, Kazancı DD, et al.

TÜRKÇE

Once labeled the "Dark Ages" of Anatolian prehistory, the Chalcolithic period (c. 6000-3000 BCE) was characterized by drastically reduced settlement densities compared with the preceding Neolithic and the succeeding Bronze Ages, a change interpreted as population decline. Using 44 new genomes combined with published data, we find no evidence for a population reduction. The genetic evidence instead suggests that populations in Chalcolithic Anatolia were increasingly mobile and/or growing in size while receiving gene flow from eastern regions (e.g., East Anatolia and the South Caucasus) through a 3,000-year-long admixture process.
