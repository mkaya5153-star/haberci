Autoantibody origins in lupus and upon recurrence post CAR-T therapy.Autoantibody origins in lupus and upon recurrence post CAR-T therapy.

Cell · Grenov A, Yoon J, Samelis VA, et al.

TÜRKÇE

Anti-CD19 chimeric antigen receptor (CAR)-T therapy induces profound remissions in lupus by depleting B cells, challenging the view that disease is sustained by long-lived plasma cells. Using molecular antibody tagging in mouse models of monogenic childhood-onset lupus, we show that autoantibody-producing plasma cells are short-lived and require continuous replenishment from T-bet-dependent precursors derived from long-lived B cell cohorts. The spleen is a major plasma cell reservoir, with perivascular enrichment observed in patients with lupus. Both anti-CD19 and anti-B-cell maturation antigen (BCMA) CAR-T therapies eliminate anti-DNA antibodies.
